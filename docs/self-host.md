# Run your own service

You can run the scope Worker on your own Cloudflare account or on one Mac.
The scope Worker serves both the API and the room page. Choose the page
for your host:

- [Run your own service on Cloudflare](self-host-cloudflare.md): deploy
  the scope Worker on your own Cloudflare account, create a room, and
  invite a person on another device.
- [Run your own service on a Mac](self-host-mac.md): run SeaweedFS, celld
  and Caddy as LaunchDaemons under one user on one Mac.

After the first deployment, [Operate a service](operate-a-service.md)
gives the steps that keep the service running, including a change of
register.
