Companion to `notes/2026-10-04-scope-and-replay-contract.md`, revision 12
("the contract"; section numbers below are its own unless a row says
R2 or R3). Design only. This file is part of the contract. As
revision 11 had it, it is adopted with the contract, as evidence that
the contract's forms can express the lane definitions. That adoption
did not make it R2's definitions. What revision 12 changes in it is
listed below, and is proposed until revision 12 is adopted.

This file writes the two lane definitions of the lane design, `issue`
and `change`, in the contract's forms: every item type, act, receive
handler and timed rule. It is the evidence for the rows of the
contract's section 15 that say a form is adopted or that a need is
already expressible. It is not a second definition of the lanes. R2
owns the definitions, and where this file and R2 differ, the note
below the row says so and R2 decides.

- **Chains of timed rules.** Every chain here is 1. `hold-end` leaves
  a hold `ended`, which is final. `job-deadline` leaves a job
  `timed-out`, which no timed rule lists. So a hold and a job each
  reserve 1 entry for their deadline, as the table has it.
- **Clauses that start a duty.** Three sends have a clause with
  effects. The `create` of `add-concern` has the second row above.
  Its `refused` and `conflict` clauses leave the concern in a final
  state. The `refused` clause of `reserve`, in `merge`, leaves the
  merge `aborted` or `refused`, which are final. The `undelivered`
  clause of `withdraw`, in `cancel-merge`, empties one value slot.
  Neither starts a duty. No clause sets a state that a timed rule
  lists.
- **A request that a handler refuses.** The deciding entry is written
  and keeps what was read before the refusal. It is new work (section
  17.3), and it draws on no row of this table. An `unpin` that is
  refused `not-the-owner` keeps 2 foreign entries, its source entry
  and the manifest, and leaves the pin and its reservation as they
  were (witness 18.18). There is one exception, by the capability's
  declaration, and it is new in revision 11. The first decision on an
  `export-license` request that is bound to a standing receiver pin
  draws on the entry that the pin reserved for its number, also when
  the decision is a refusal (sections 6.11 and 17.3; witness 18.20).
