# Wait a bounded time at S1's stage-ready and published phases

Status: source implementation. The gate passed at `e7081bf6`. No room has run the wait yet. No hosted code changes.

Request `8ea6bc6add244b6c957fb0ea1b44065a6573b743` and promise `efb308af21b30d22d294d46b641c23c7778d0365` own this head.

## What changed

The `stage-ready` and `published` phases of `examples/repo-in-ai` now wait for a pending read before they stop.

- **The wait.** `whenSettled` in `examples/repo-in-ai/publication.ts` repeats the phase's read up to 15 times, 2 seconds apart, in `new` and `continue` modes. The bound is 30 seconds of pauses, plus the time of the 16 reads.
- **A stop.** A read that stays pending stops the phase with the same words and exit code as before. `check` then recovers the phase, as it did in the real room.
- **`check`.** It reads once and does not pause.
- **A failed read.** It stops the phase at once, with its own words.
- **Runbook.** `examples/repo-in-ai/runbook.md` states the wait, the bound and the recovery.

## Decisions

- **Choose 15 rechecks, 2 seconds apart.** The real room's two stops give the only measurement, in the table below. The 30 seconds of pauses are 2.5 times the larger upper limit, and they stay under the read session's re-ask at about 50 seconds. A `published` attempt makes at least seven requests, so 2 seconds spaces them more than the inbox's 1 second would.
- **`check` does not wait.** This matches the enrollment recheck and `claim.ts`. `new` has already used the wait by the time a person runs `check`.
- **Add no recheck count to the receipt.** The enrollment receipt has `inboxRechecks`, and these two phases have no such field. A later run's receipt cannot show how long the wait took. I left the field out to keep the change small.

## Open limits

- **The wait has not run live.** No room or provider ran this code. Whether 30 seconds covers a later room's window is unknown. If it does not, the phase stops and `check` recovers it.
- **"Signs no act" is the claim, not "signs nothing".** A read can sign a read-session request, and if membership refuses a session, each read is signed. T6 shows one `POST /sessions` and no acts, but its clock moves only by the pauses. A wait with slow reads could cross 50 s and sign one more session request.
- **30 seconds bounds the pauses, not the total time.** Each request also has its own 30-second reply limit in `http.ts`. Nobody measured the read time.
- **A pending read and a refused stage look the same.** A stage that the room refused also uses the whole bound, and then stops with the same words.
- **A lane head that moves between pages stops the phase at once.** `laneState` throws, as it did before, and `check` recovers the phase.
- **Reads can add local records.** Each read retains a private `s1r-` record in the owner's home, under a name derived from its content. A recheck that reads something new adds a record.
- **What the tests do not cover.** `publication.test.ts` scripts the lane fold. T6 runs the real session and fold, on a lane that stays pending. No test settles a real hold outcome, and no test runs through `main`.
