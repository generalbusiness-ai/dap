The default production budget records admitted debit deltas at public
source coordinates. It keeps at most 64 sites, 16 refusals, two frames
per site, and the first, selected and last completed quotes. Direct
charges use their own location; the shared SQL wrapper also records its
immediate caller when available. Unknown sources, overflow, malformed
geometry and failed reconciliation remain explicit. Custom budgets keep
only unavailable capture metadata: no row, quote, selection or terminal
geometry is retained for them.

The existing plan supplies row and row-context sizes, native entry and
reference geometry, source lookup cost, parent object/byte totals,
preparation counters and component vectors. Each known target sequence
and immutable hash comes from the existing native head. The observer
copies only closed numeric fields, booleans, fixed labels and that explicit
hash. It retains no bodies, signatures, tokens, headers, settings, SQL,
object content or private parent path names. It adds no endpoint, source
read, row serialization, receipt consumption or forecast call.

The original budget still owns every admission, limit and clock check.
Its original native methods receive the same charge object once. An
internal `canSpend` call inside `spend` is not counted twice. A custom
factory is called once and retains its budget object. Observation errors
cannot replace the original archive result or exception. Stack inspection
and allocations can advance the original real-time deadline; no allowance
is added for them.

A frozen snapshot at the first completed quote explains the **pre-finish**
ledger. Inventory, layout, preparation and one entry can already have
charged work at that point; it is not a pre-entry baseline. Later writer
charges and overflow cannot rewrite that snapshot. Complete first-finish
geometry and attribution do not imply complete terminal attribution,
source authority or a successful archive. Failed forecasts do not become
completed quotes.
