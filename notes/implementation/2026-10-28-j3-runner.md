## Decisions and why

The measured nerdctl exec path changed status 17 into status 1 and appended
a diagnostic. The adapter uses ctr for execution and judges the last stdout
line. Both streams count against the output bound. Abnormal close, status
255, or nonzero status with stderr remains lost because setup and transport
failure cannot be distinguished from job output at that boundary. Nonzero
status with empty stderr remains judgeable. A job may combine its streams
inside its configured shell. Printed report JSON is always job output.

Eligible results require physical container removal after the attached
lifecycle child closes, followed by a fresh absence check. For legacy reads,
the owner also stops admission, aborts and joins owned request, forward, and
body work, closes the read-only Gateway grant, and writes an ask-bound durable closure
acknowledgement. The supervisor and record reader both require it. Late or
uncooperative cleanup leaves a lost run. Request cancellation crosses the
Gateway's rebuilt Request through the Node wrapper's async context.

The owning Native proposal scenario uses the actual adapter only through
the explicit acceptance driver. Its passing process reads the actual
checked-out proposal, the service signs outside the container, and the lane
publishes exactly the reservation tree. A second proposal fails the same
configuration and leaves the previous head unchanged. Wrong-tree results
are refused. The ordinary Native suite retains its labelled runner stand-in.
The explicit mode retains TEST host, scheduler, activation, configuration,
and durability boundaries. It proves no hosted service or provider outcome.

Eight direct actual-container cases cover exact tree and read-only Gateway,
custody and network denial with a positive network control, private writes,
real failure, exact status 17, missing executable, hostile PATH and forged
output, wrong tree, fresh filesystems, and finite termination. They also
check retained recovery, changed-job refusal, record modes, container absence,
origin closure, and zero Gateway updates. Several properties share one case.

Four focused controls distinguish origin ownership, identity-bound recovery,
claim size before reservation, and setup-error provenance. In the last
control, one actual snapshot job passes the witness; deleting only the guard
produces a compiled mutant whose actual report incorrectly records a
completed status-1 step. The witness fails its `complete` versus `lost`
assertion. The source is restored before the collector closes. The earlier
inconclusive claim control remains separately retained.
