## What changed

The deployed host refuses fresh signed acts under an installed release that
is absent from current `APPLICATION_TRUST`. The answer is
`application-revoked`. Missing or malformed trust also closes this admission.
Native creation records identify the exact release for the installed root
and its declared descendants. Two releases can share every definition digest
without sharing this decision.

Admission runs before new foreign dependency reads and is checked again
before writing an act. Previously accepted keys bypass new admission and
retain their receipt or mismatch answer. A refused genesis keeps its existing
`scope-refused` judgment. Replay, deliveries, timed rules, outcomes, and
capability preparations keep their recorded rules. In particular, an
already-open device command can still use its reserved answer step after
withdrawal.

The contract, transport codec, metric reason tables, and MCP explanation name
the new refusal. Regenerated page and MCP resources contain the new refusal
entry and updated digests. Their presentation and policies are unchanged. Architecture, scope, entry-point, setting, and deployment
references describe its ownership. Native fixtures now supply explicit,
lifetime-owned trust through their actual object environment.
