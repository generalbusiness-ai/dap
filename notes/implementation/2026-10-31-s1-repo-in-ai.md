## What changed

A person can now create the room that slices S2 to S9 use, seed it and witness it with one script.

- **Setup.** `examples/repo-in-ai/setup.mjs` runs 42 phases, in the order of `setupPhases` in `setup.ts`: owner and room, three enrollments, definitions, role lists, rules, a seed Change, a hold, a workspace push, a manifest, two reviews, a merge, a fresh clone comparison and a release. `runbook.md` is the witness script.
- **Seed.** `examples/repo-in-ai/task-list/` is a small task list app with add, toggle and browser persistence. Clear-completed stays a later product decision.
- **Checker key invitations.** `packages/client/src/checker-key-invitation.ts` adds a separate link format. `packages/cli/src/invitations.ts` adds `issueCheckerInvitation` and `enrolCheckerKey`, and `dap join` accepts the link. A checker holds native kind and role `checker` and no controller.
- **Retained originals.** The CLI claim and invitation engines keep the exact original before transport, and the example flushes each retained file and its directory before dispatch. `check` settles an original and signs nothing. `continue` reuses the same envelope and deadline.
- **Retained clone.** `cloneRetained` in `packages/cli/src/commands.ts` spends an accepted read-token request without signing another act. An exclusive marker fences the one-time credential.
- **Diagnostics.** Every invocation needs a new `--diagnostics` directory, which holds fixed stages and causes and no body, key or link.
- **Test wiring.** `examples/repo-in-ai` has its own Vitest project and typecheck. `scripts/security-entry-points.test.mjs` seals the changed entry points. `apps/room/page/assets.ts` is regenerated.
