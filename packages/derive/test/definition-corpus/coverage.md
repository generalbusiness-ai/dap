- `packages/contract/test/shapes.ts#definition` is a compile-shape composite. Its `link` type has `many: true`, while `acts.close.also.last` uses `one: true`. The format-1 `also()` check rejects that pairing. It is not a positive declaration.
- `packages/bytes/test/fixtures.ts#definition` and MCP `errors.test.ts` and `wire.test.ts` scripted schemas have empty acts and a named genesis without an opening act. They are presentation or transport stand-ins.
- MCP `restricted@2`, malformed or future guard stand-ins, and partial `DefinitionLike` values have no positive native format-1 admission claim.
- MCP `declared-view-inputs.test.ts` with `ordered: true` and both `declared-view-routing.test.ts` `withMembers` variants are unchecked presentation fixtures. Their original declarations change the board to `many: true` but inherit `also.board.one: true` constraints. That pairing is invalid under the existing format-1 check. The three rows are excluded without changing their original helpers, presentation tests, validator, or pins. The `ordered: false` input declaration remains registered.
- CLI listing names containing control characters and the inactive story object are presentation-only inputs. They are not successful native declarations.
- Graph future-form P4 states, P1 reads, P2 spacing, P3 timed sends, and S3 sequenced fixtures explicitly disable native validation. Their exact exclusions remain in the graph ledger.
- Integrity-corrupted package bytes, invalid validator-table rows, and malformed declaration mutations remain negative controls in their original owners.
- Entries, seeds, observation records, projection rows, rendered fields, rule result overrides, and runtime state mutations are not new declared definitions.
- Fixed validator bounds, profiles, and options require separate rows. Bounds calculated from actual history sizes or post-construction quota measurements remain owning history witnesses with exact source locators. The corpus does not invent their numeric values or claim to execute those histories. A subset of a measured bound tuple is not registered with default values in place of the measurements. Replay child `namedDefinitions: 2` contexts with measured bytes remain in their owning histories. Scope keeps only the complete fixed child and genesis tuples that actual callers validate; unused extra definitions and the absent legacy leaf do not acquire Native aliases.
